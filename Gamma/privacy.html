NuclearThreatWatch.org is owned and operated by DigiMktg Co. LLC, a limited liability company registered in Illinois. For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, "we," "us," and "our" refer to DigiMktg Co. LLC. "You" and "your" refer to any person who accesses or uses NuclearThreatWatch.org.

Questions about this policy can be directed to privacy@nuclearthreatwatch.org.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

The only way NuclearThreatWatch.org actively collects information from you is through the contact form on the Contact page. If you submit the form, you may provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message and selected topic
  • Whether you opted in to the DigiMktg Co. mailing list

This information is transmitted to and stored by Formspree (formspree.io), our third-party form processing service, and forwarded to our team email inbox. We use this information solely to read, respond to, and organize your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share contact form submissions with any third party for marketing purposes.

Mailing List

If you check the mailing list opt-in box on the contact form, your name and email address may be added to a DigiMktg Co. LLC communications list. You may opt out at any time by emailing privacy@nuclearthreatwatch.org with the subject line "Unsubscribe."

Information Collected Automatically

Like virtually all websites, NuclearThreatWatch.org and its infrastructure providers may automatically collect certain technical information when you visit, including:

  • IP address — collected by Cloudflare (our CDN and DNS provider) for security, performance, and abuse prevention purposes
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URL — the page you came from before visiting this site
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Date and time of access

This information is collected at the infrastructure level by Cloudflare and, if analytics are enabled, by Google Analytics or a similar service. See Section 5 for details on analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above for the following purposes:

  • To respond to contact form inquiries
  • To send communications to mailing list subscribers who have opted in
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website
  • To monitor and prevent abuse, spam, and security threats
  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, profiling, or any purpose not listed here without updating this policy first.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

NuclearThreatWatch.org may use cookies and similar tracking technologies. Here is a breakdown by source:

Cloudflare

Cloudflare may set a cookie (__cf_bm and similar) for bot management and security purposes. These are functional cookies necessary for the secure operation of the site. Cloudflare's privacy policy is available at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Google AdSense

This site displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to NuclearThreatWatch.org and other sites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Amazon Associates

NuclearThreatWatch.org participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. When you click an Amazon affiliate link on this site, Amazon may set cookies on your device to track purchases for commission attribution purposes. Amazon's privacy policy is available at amazon.com.

Google Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may log your IP address when fonts are requested. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Analytics

This site may use Google Analytics or a similar analytics platform to understand aggregate visitor behavior. If enabled, analytics cookies may track pages visited, session duration, and general geographic region (not precise location). We do not use analytics data to identify individual users. If you wish to opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites, you may use the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-On.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — Third-party services listed in Section 5 that process data on our behalf as necessary to operate the site
  • Legal compliance — If required by law, court order, or valid government request
  • Protection of rights — To protect the rights, property, or safety of DigiMktg Co. LLC, our users, or the public
  • Business transfer — In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via a prominent notice on this page if such a transfer occurs.

7. Data Retention

Contact form submissions forwarded to our inbox are retained as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain records of communications. Formspree retains submission data according to their own retention policy. Mailing list subscriber information is retained until you unsubscribe.

Server and infrastructure logs maintained by Cloudflare are governed by Cloudflare's own retention schedule. We do not maintain separate server-side access logs beyond what Cloudflare captures.

8. Your Rights — U.S. Residents

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to know — You may request information about what personal data we hold about you
  • Right to deletion — You may request that we delete personal information we hold about you, subject to certain exceptions
  • Right to opt out of sale — We do not sell personal information, so this right is not applicable
  • Right to correct — You may request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination — We will not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy rights

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). Illinois residents may have rights under the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@nuclearthreatwatch.org. We will respond within 45 days of a verified request.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal information through our contact form, please contact us at privacy@nuclearthreatwatch.org and we will delete that information promptly.
