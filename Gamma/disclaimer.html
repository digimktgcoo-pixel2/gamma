3. Data Accuracy, Completeness, and Timeliness

Radiation data displayed on NuclearThreatWatch.org is sourced from the EPA's publicly available CDX RadNet API endpoint. DigiMktg Co. LLC has no control over the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, or availability of data provided by the EPA or any other third-party data source. We make no warranty, express or implied, that any data displayed on this website is:

  • Accurate — Data is transmitted from EPA sensors and may contain errors, instrument malfunctions, calibration gaps, or transmission artifacts.
  • Complete — Not all RadNet stations report at all times. Station outages, maintenance windows, and transmission failures can result in missing data for any station at any time.
  • Current — While the target update cadence is hourly, actual posting times vary. Data labeled "Stale" or "Estimated" explicitly indicates reduced freshness. Even data labeled "Live" reflects the most recent available posting — not necessarily a reading taken within the past hour.
  • Representative of your location — RadNet stations are spaced miles apart. A reading from the nearest station does not represent the radiation level at your specific location. Local variations, weather, geography, and proximity to radiation sources all affect readings that no fixed regional sensor can capture for your exact address.

Estimated and fallback values displayed when live data is unavailable are synthetic placeholders. They are clearly labeled as such and should under no circumstances be used to make any safety, health, or emergency decision. They exist solely to maintain interface usability during data outages.
